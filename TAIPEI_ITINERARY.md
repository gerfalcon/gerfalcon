# The PERFECT 3 Days in Taipei Itinerary

## Day 1: Culture and Night Markets

### Morning – Enjoy An Early Morning Stroll Around Tamsui
Begin your time in Taipei by getting out of the city to the lovely coastal area of **Tamsui**. It’s littered with temples, old markets, and plenty of great places to pick up a bite to eat when the sun has risen.
*   **Must see:** Hobe Fort, Aletheia University, Peace Park.
*   **Explore:** The maze of alleyways made of markets, shops, and temples to the north-west of the MRT.

### Afternoon – Palace Museum
Visiting the **Palace Museum** should be high on your list! This museum contains over 700,000 pieces of Chinese art encompassing 8000 years of Chinese history. The sheer quantity and diversity of what’s on show is staggering.
*   **Tips:** Plan what you want to see first. The calligraphy section is particularly impressive.
*   **Getting there:** Head to Shilin MRT, take exit 1, and then take bus R30, minibus 18 or 19, or bus 255, 304 or 815.
*   **Opening Hours:** 8:30 am to 6:30 pm (extended to 21:00 on Fri/Sat).

### Evening – Shilin Night Market
**Shilin Night Market** is Taipei’s biggest and most famous night market. Expect plenty of market stalls offering all kinds of Taiwanese delicacies.
*   **Getting there:** Short walk from Shilin station.
*   **Highlights:** Taiwanese street food, temples buried in the mazes.

### Night – Long Shan Temple and Nearby Night Market
This temple in the south of the city is one of Taipei’s most stunning and popular temples. It is open 24 hours and is particularly atmospheric at night.
*   **Highlights:** The temple at night, the nearby open-air night market which is more relaxed than Shilin.
*   **Getting there:** Longshan Temple MRT.

## Day 2: History and City Views

### Morning – Chiang Kai Shek’s Memorial Hall
The structure occupies one end of a large square in central Taipei’s Zhong Zheng District. The changing of the guard’s ceremony that happens every hour is particularly interesting.
*   **Getting there:** Chiang Kai Shek Memorial Hall MRT.
*   **Opening Hours:** 9:00 am until 6:00 pm every day.

### Afternoon – Explore the Area Around Dongmen
Home to some of the city's most famous beef noodle restaurants, particularly on **Yongkang Street**. Nearby is the lovely **Da’an Park** where you can escape the concrete.

### Afternoon – Sun Yat Sen’s Memorial Hall
A short subway ride away is Sun Yat Sen’s Mausoleum. The museum here is particularly interesting and demonstrates Sun’s importance to the Chinese people.
*   **Getting there:** Sun Yat-Sen Memorial Hall MRT.

### Evening – Elephant Hill (Xiangshan)
Climb **Elephant Hill** for one of the most stunning night views of the city.
*   **Tip:** Get to the Xiangshan subway station at least an hour before sunset to catch the best views as the sun sets and the night sky envelops the city.
*   **Getting there:** Xiangshan MRT.

### Night – Taipei 101
Once the tallest building in the world, **Taipei 101** towers over the Taipei skyline. The area around it is a mixture of malls and restaurants.
*   **Highlights:** Observation deck (9 am to 10 pm daily), exhibition on the tuned mass damper.
*   **Getting there:** Taipei 101/World Trade Centre MRT.

## Day 3: Nature and Mountains

### Morning – Wulai
Nestled amongst the mountains outside of Taipei, **Wulai** is a gorgeous town with temples, hot springs, and hiking.
*   **Highlights:** Soaking in hot springs, Wulai Waterfall, walking trails.
*   **Getting there:** MRT to Xindian station, then bus 849 (approx 15 mins).

### Afternoon – Maokong
Famous for its tea plantations and gondola ride.
*   **Highlights:** Take the **Maokong Gondola** from Taipei Zoo. Enjoy the views, visit tea plantations, and walk to the silver stream waterfall.
*   **Getting there:** MRT to Taipei Zoo, then Maokong Gondola.

### Evening – Raohe Night Market and Sung Shan Tzu Yu Temple
End your trip with a visit to the gorgeous **Sung Shan Tzu Yu Temple** and the **Raohe Night Market**.
*   **Highlights:** The temple at night, excellent food at the night market (one of the quieter ones).
*   **Getting there:** Songshan MRT.

---

## Practical Tips
*   **Transport:** Taipei has an excellent MRT (subway) system. Consider a 3-day unlimited rail pass or an EasyCard.
*   **Weather:** Taipei can be hot and humid. Bring comfortable shoes.
*   **Food:** Don't miss Din Tai Fung for Xiaolongbao (soup dumplings), beef noodles on Yongkang Street, and various street foods at night markets.
